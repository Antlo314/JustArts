OUR MISSION

Just Arts, Inc.

Just Arts, Inc. empowers youth ages 10-adult through high-quality arts and culinary education programs that foster awareness, creativity, and leadership.

Participants

Participants develop artistic and culinary skills, enhance self-expression, build teamwork and leadership abilities, and gain advocacy skills that support personal growth and future success.

Offers

By offering accessible, inclusive programming, Just Arts cultivates the next generation of creative and community leaders while strengthening connections within underserved communities.

PROUDLY PARTNERED WITH

Collisa Lankford

CEO & Founder, Motivational Speaker, Educator & Facilitator

Collisa Lankford is a dedicated educator, community leader, and the visionary founder of Just Arts, Inc. A native of Queens, New York, she has spent her career inspiring marginalized youth through artistic expression and creative programming across multiple states. Her deep commitment to equity and empowerment ensures that every young person she reaches has the tools and support to succeed and make their voices heard.

Currently serving as the Executive Director and Creative Director of the Athens Area Performing Arts Collective, Collisa continues to foster inclusive environments where the arts thrive and communities are strengthened. She lives by the words of Max DePree: "The first responsibility of a leader is to define reality. The last is to say thank you. In between, the leader is a servant."

COMMON QUESTIONS

Who can participate in Just Arts programs?

+

Our programs are primarily designed for youth and marginalized voices in the Athens, GA community. Age ranges vary by specific program, typically from 12 to 18 years old.

Is there a cost for the workshops?

+

Most of our core community workshops are provided at low or no cost to participants, thanks to our generous sponsors and donors. Some specialized intensive programs may have a nominal materials fee.

Where are the classes held?

+

Classes are held at our primary hub, Echo Base (147 Fritz Mar Lane), and at various partner community centers across Athens.

How can I volunteer or mentor?

+

We are always looking for passionate artists and community leaders! Please use the contact form below and select "General Inquiry" to express your interest in volunteering.
